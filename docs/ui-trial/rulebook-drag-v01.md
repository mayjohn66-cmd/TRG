# Rulebook drag scrolling and full-width cards — 2026-10-28

Both rulebook scroll panes accept a held mouse/finger drag anywhere in their content, including over a card. An 8-screen-pixel movement threshold separates a tap from a drag. The gesture consumes the press/release before child buttons can select a card, so a swipe does not open a detail by accident. A short tap opens its original entry. Native touch owns one finger; emulated mouse events and extra fingers cannot scroll the same gesture twice. Wheel input, the actual scrollbar, chapter buttons and keyboard selection retain their existing paths. The modal blocks dragging the grid behind it, while its long real card rules scroll independently.

The rounded book now follows the available landscape canvas width instead of keeping a centred 1366px strip on wider phone aspects. Its inset, summary, header controls, content pane and modal dim/centre resize together. Original left tabs stay above the modal dim. Cards fill their grid columns and set each row's minimum height from the uniformly scaled original face; their image, border and hit area agree without stretching artwork or leaving an empty right-hand strip. Phones use three columns for augment-based entries and four for portrait playing cards. Desktop keeps four columns. No new art, colours or game rules.
