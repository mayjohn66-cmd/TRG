# Black-stone alkkagi presentation

The black Go stone's one-use skill now opens an overhead wooden alkkagi board instead of drawing a screen-space line over the isometric map. Press anywhere on that board, drag backward and release to shoot in the opposite direction. Mouse and primary-touch gestures share the same coordinate conversion; a tiny drag, Escape, right click, the cancel button, a cancelled OS touch, focus loss, and resize do not fire a shot.

The aiming view shows glossy black/white stones with player-color rims, a segmented power meter, dotted travel preview, eligible-target rings, hit count, shield indication, and final landing tile. Its coordinates directly match `BoardLayout` and `PawnSkills.shot_result`, so a camera tilt cannot rotate the aim away from the actual hit line. The shooter has a brief pullback, fast initial slide with friction-like deceleration, and a short fading trail. Contacts occur in spatial order at the combined stone radius, with a dry stone click, small burst and a 65 ms local hold. Victims slide outward and fall away. The existing board-settlement and capture queue resumes after the table presentation ends.

`AlkkagiStage` renders and times the presentation; it never changes pawn state, computes new game outcomes, samples gameplay RNG, or changes `Engine.time_scale`. The authoritative skill's distance, eligible victims, immunity, captures, landing and one-use consumption remain in the existing engine. Its visual collision response follows those resolved hits; it does not add a new free-form rebound or chain-capture rule. Remote and bot skill events use the same snapshot and presentation path. The existing screen-shake setting controls the stage's small impact shake, and its speed follows the game's animation pace.

The stage uses a CanvasLayer below capture poses. The generic blocking skill announcement is omitted for alkkagi so releasing the pointer leads directly into the flick. Reset or rewind cancels the stage and its held input safely. The obsolete isometric aiming line and generic straight-line tween have been removed.

Validation: `tests/ui_alkkagi_test.gd` covers coordinate direction, preview/engine agreement, allied and shielded exclusions, one-shot mouse release, tiny drags, normal and cancelled touch, spatial contact order, local hit-stop, deceleration, a miss, natural FIFO completion, and reset. Native 1366×768 and 844×390 captures and a recorded demo are in workspace `outputs/alkkagi-v01`. The existing UI smoke and full UI FX suites also pass locally. CI runs the focused alkkagi test before deployment.
